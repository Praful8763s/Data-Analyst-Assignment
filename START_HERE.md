## 🔥 Pro Tips

1. **Spend 80% of your time reading/understanding, 20% executing.**
   - Most candidates do the opposite. Don't.

2. **On voice notes: Speak naturally, not formally.**
   - "So ROAS is basically..." is better than "ROAS is defined as..."

3. **Show your work.**
   - Prove you understand by showing step-by-step reasoning, not just final answers.

4. **Different stakeholders, different language.**
   - Finance person cares about profit margins. MD cares about revenue growth. Brand manager cares about ROAS.

5. **Test your model with extreme scenarios.**
   - What if ad spend is ₹0? What if COGS doubles? Does it still work?

6. **Read the assignment multiple times.**
   - First read: understand scope
   - Second read: identify what to learn
   - Third read: see connections between tasks
